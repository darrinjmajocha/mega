How to play Call It

Call both colors before tossing. Each matching side earns 1 point. Earn 1 more if another player rolls your exact pair. Lowest score calls first. If you copy an earlier call, miss it, and that player rolls it, the bonus becomes −1. Editing a call gives it a new place in the claim order.

A green side earns 3 points, or 9 if you called it. Double green wins the game.
